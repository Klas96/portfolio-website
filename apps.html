Echoes of Elysium

A calm story adventure about AI, nature, and the memory of a lost world.

In the year 2387, AI engineer Dr. Kaela Osei awakens Gaia, the consciousness of the planet Elysium, which holds the memories of the Aetherians, an ancient people who chose harmony over conquest. When the United Earth Coalition moves to erase Gaia's first memory, Kaela sets out to protect it. Explore painted woods, cities, and ruins, uncover fragments of the Aetherian past, and enjoy a gentle adventure with no game over and an optional Story Mode.

Android: add https://klas96.github.io/fdroid/repo in the F-Droid app (or tap here on your phone), then search for Echoes of Elysium.
