Echoes of Elysium

A story of AI, nature, and the echoes of a lost world

Project Overview

A calm story adventure about AI, nature, and the memory of a lost world.

In the year 2387, AI engineer Dr. Kaela Osei awakens Gaia, the consciousness of the planet Elysium, which holds the memories of the Aetherians, an ancient people who chose harmony over conquest. When the United Earth Coalition moves to erase Gaia's first memory, Kaela sets out to protect it. Explore painted woods, cities, and ruins, uncover fragments of the Aetherian past, and enjoy a gentle adventure with no game over and an optional Story Mode.

Key Features

  • Top-down 2D story RPG set on the planet Elysium
  • Explore painted woods, cities, and ruins
  • Uncover fragments of the Aetherian past
  • Gentle by design: no game over, plus an optional Story Mode
  • Music and voiced characters

Play in your browser

The full game runs right here in the browser. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, E to interact, Esc to pause. The first load downloads the game's music and graphics, so it can take a moment.

Install on Android (F-Droid)

Echoes of Elysium is distributed through my own F-Droid repository.

  1. Install the F-Droid app.
  2. In F-Droid, open Settings → Repositories, tap + and add https://klas96.github.io/fdroid/repo (or scan the QR code / tap the link below on your phone).
  3. Refresh, then search for Echoes of Elysium and install it.
